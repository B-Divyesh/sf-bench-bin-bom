Privacy

Bench Bin BOM stores parts, optional photos, project BOMs, and license tokens on your device. It has no analytics or tracking.

License checks

When you add or restore a Bench Pass, the app sends only that token to Sociobot. It caches the result for one day.

Files and deletion

CSV files leave the app only when you export them. Clear the app storage or uninstall it to remove local data.
